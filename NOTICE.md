# Attribution

CLIProxyBar derives from the menu bar presentation and domain models in
[Quotio](https://github.com/nguyenphutrong/quotio), by Trong Nguyen.
The extraction started from commit `e0ad2858e8cf25240f7736c955491004288dc12c`.
The original MIT copyright and license remain in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

CLIProxyBar adds a standalone CLIProxyAPI client, active quota requests,
cache support, compact menus, pace displays, settings, and OpenCode Go quotas.
CLIProxyBar is an independent project. It has no affiliation with CLIProxyAPI
or the providers that appear in the menu.

Provider names and logos belong to their respective owners. The provider
artwork in `Assets.xcassets` comes from Quotio. The CLIProxyBar app icon is
original artwork generated by `scripts/generate-icon.swift`.
